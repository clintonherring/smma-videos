# Which videos, and when

The one place that answers "what do we make next". `08-video-types.md` says what kind of video a concept is; this file says **which one to pick, why now, where it goes, and how we know it worked**. The business's own answers live in `pillars.md`, `calendar.md`, `seeds.md` and `channels.md`; `video-schedule` and `make-video` read them and report the answer as the "Why now" line in the brief summary.

Structure adapted from the pillar / phase / gap planning in `prateekjain98/social-media-manager-skills` (the video-related parts only; see `reference/sources.md`). The calendar is the business's own.

## 1. Pillars

The planning unit. A pillar has one kind of viewer, one job, a share of what gets made, and a way to tell whether it is working. Three to five of them. Topics sit under pillars; a topic is not a video until it has a pillar, a slot and a viewer's moment.

| Pillar | For | What it is meant to do | Share | Working when | Backlog IDs (first sort) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| e.g. **Workshop knowledge** | the peer | teach one check or one rule they can use before the expensive part is gone | ~40 % | the commuter group shares a video unprompted; a customer says "I checked it like in the video" | V01, V03, V04, V05 |
| e.g. **Show the part** | the peer | show the worn part and the number, so "they upsell me" stops being what they believe | ~30 % | a comment or message that names the part; a booking that mentions the video | V02, V06, V08 |
| e.g. **Come in** | the peer and the hesitant first-timer | make turning up feel easy: hours, prices on the wall, no judgement | ~20 % | Sunday checks counted; bookings with `?from=V##` | V07 |
| e.g. **Why it exists** | people deciding | context and trust; long form and LinkedIn | ~10 % | decision-maker conversations; long-form completion past the limits chapter | none yet; long form in phase 3 |

How pillars get written (`define-pillars`): **as a sort of the existing backlog**, not as categories invented in the abstract. The guide reads the seeded ideas, groups them by viewer and job, names the groups, and proposes shares and "working when" marked `[Owner]`. A pillar with no ideas under it is a sign the pillar or the concept is not clear yet.

Rules:

- One pillar per video. If a video serves two, pick the one whose viewer it is written for; the other is the cross-over.
- Shares are a period's mix, not a weekly quota. Check them at the review (§8), not per video.
- When a pillar is under its share and has material available, it gets the next slot.
- A new concept with no pillar is a sign the concept is not clear yet, or that a pillar is missing. Say which.

## 2. Calendar slots

The business's own rhythm decides what to make and when, better than a posting quota does: a season, a buying cycle, key dates, launches, or, failing all of those, a plain cadence the person can sustain. `calendar.md` holds the slots; each backlog row carries one.

| Slot | When | What is happening | What to make | What to publish |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| e.g. **Before** | the quiet months before the season | people plan and budget | evergreen how-tos from last year's material; group-facing videos; the signature-line library | workshop knowledge and come-in shorts, so they are findable when people start looking |
| e.g. **Peak** | the busy months | most work, least time | shoot, do not edit: the walkthrough day, stories, "one job, one number" | short stories cut quickly from the week's footage |
| e.g. **After** | when the numbers exist | retrospectives | origin, offer, decision-maker cuts | long forms; LinkedIn |
| **Cadence** (no season) | every week / fortnight | — | one short per slot, pillars alternating | — |

Rules:

- Shoot in season, publish out of season when you can. Footage is the scarce thing; editing time is not.
- Any video that needs footage from the busy period sits in that slot. Do not brief one for a quiet slot unless the footage already exists in `assets/ASSETS.md`.
- The slot before the season is the deadline for anything people would search for at its start.

## 3. Phases (cold start)

Nobody is subscribed yet. Reach has to be borrowed before it is owned. Exit criteria are plain numbers the owner can see; the values are proposals `[Owner]` until confirmed.

| Phase | What we make | Where it goes | Exit when |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Borrowed reach** | evergreen knowledge; the thing in use; one group-facing video | existing communities, groups, direct sends to people the owner already knows; own channels as the archive | N videos published; each posted into at least 2 places; a number of sign-ups / bookings / "same problem" comments attributable to a video; one conversation that started from a video |
| **2. A series** | the recurring format with a spine (§5) | own channels now carry some weight; still shared into groups | N episodes; shares ≥ 10 % of views on two; one enquiry naming the series; a viewer asking "when is the next one" |
| **3. Long form and the people who decide** | walkthrough, origin, decision-maker cut, offer | YouTube; LinkedIn; direct sends | one long form with completion past the limits chapter above 30 %; one decision-maker conversation referencing a video; price questions answered by the offer video instead of in comments |

Rules:

- The phase gates which backlog rows are *eligible*; pillar share and slot pick among them.
- Phase 3 videos may be shot in Phase 1 (the walkthrough needs a busy day) but are edited and published when their phase arrives.
- Roadmap-gated rows sit outside the phases until the feature works.

## 4. Saturation filter

Whatever the business does, the internet already has the generic version of it. Prefer the under-served column when two candidates are otherwise equal. `seeds.md` ("Content gaps") holds the business's own version of this table.

| Saturated | Under-served |
|---|---|
| cinematic footage of the thing | what a job costs: hours, minutes, people, parts |
| the "look at this result" clip with music | the post-mortem: what we found, what we missed, why |
| feature tours | a decision log: which method, and what you knew before you started |
| "our app does X" | the moment the old way failed, and what it cost |
| demo-day footage | demo vs reality: what the day did to the plan |

A video that lands in the saturated column needs a reason: a number nobody else has, or a point of view nobody else has. Otherwise it waits.

## 5. Series

One or two recurring formats so the backlog has a spine and a viewer has a reason to come back. An episode is still one video of one type; the series is the frame around it.

| Series (example) | Episode shape | Feeds pillar |
|---|---|---|
| **One job, one number** | one real job, one number on screen the whole video, and what decided it | knowledge |
| **What it cost me** | before / after on one method or setting | knowledge, the thing in use |
| **Sunday on the bench** | one real bike each week; what it needed, one number, in order | show the part |

Rules: name the series in the caption, not in the hook; a series needs three episodes planned before the first goes out, or it is just a video; series names are proposals `[Owner]`.

## 6. Distribution per video

Every brief says where the video goes and who shares it. Reach is borrowed in Phase 1, so "post it" is not a plan. `channels.md` holds the business's channels; the pattern:

| Channel | What goes there | How |
|---|---|---|
| Own channels | everything | default; poster and caption per `05-ctas-and-publishing.md` |
| Groups and communities the viewer is already in `[Owner: which]` | workshop knowledge; come-in where the rules allow | organic where rules allow a video without a pitch; guide CTA, not product CTA |
| Direct to organisations and people the owner knows `[Owner: who]` | the thing in use; stories; the walkthrough | a message with the video, not a post; the person there shares it onward |
| LinkedIn | why it exists; explainers for organisations; cuts under 3 min | one-line native caption; "talk to [owner]" |
| Language cut | anything for a group in another language | subtitles always; a voiced character variant where one exists |

Rules: pick channels at brief time; the CTA has to match the channel; one video, one primary channel.

## 7. Picking rule

What `video-schedule` and `make-video` run at intake, and what the owner can run by hand. Answer each line; the output is one sentence.

```
PICK — <ID or concept>
Pillar            <which; is it under its share this period?>
Slot              <which; does the material exist, or is a shoot needed?>
Phase             <1 / 2 / 3 / gated; is the row eligible now?>
Saturation        <saturated / under-served; if saturated, the reason it earns a slot>
Series            <episode of …, or standalone>
Distribution      <primary channel; who shares it>
Cross-over        <natural pair in BACKLOG.md, or none>
Seeds             <gap, take or story from seeds.md it draws on, or none>
Assets            <have / partial / none, from ASSETS.md; what the gap report will probably say>
→ Make this next, because <one sentence>.   (or: not yet, because <one sentence>)
```

In the brief summary this collapses to one line: `Why now   <pillar> · <slot> · phase <n> · <primary channel> · assets <have/partial/none>`.

A concept that fails three lines goes to the backlog with its slot and waits. Nothing is thrown away. A "not yet" never blocks the owner; it is said once and recorded if they go ahead.

## 8. Review

At the end of each period (`calendar.md` says when), with `PERFORMANCE.md` open:

1. Which pillar got more than its share? Less? A choice, or an accident of material?
2. Which pillar's "working when" column actually moved? Which did not move at all?
3. Which series got a "when is the next one"? Which never got a second episode?
4. What did the period give us that is not in the backlog? Add it, with a slot.
5. Did any video move the phase exit criteria? Are we in the phase we think we are in?
6. Which gap in `seeds.md` is still open? Which take did we never say on camera?
7. Which gap decisions (placeholder / text scene / leave out) hurt, and which assets would have fixed them? Request them for next period.

Write the answers at the bottom of `PERFORMANCE.md`, dated. Change the shares in `pillars.md` if the answers say to.
