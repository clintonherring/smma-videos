# Formats

## Short form (the default)

| | |
|---|---|
| Length | 20–45 s target, 60 s max (`08-video-types.md` sets the range per type) |
| Aspect | 9:16 vertical; `brand.md` may set 1:1 or 16:9 for a channel |
| Platforms | Shorts, Reels, TikTok; LinkedIn for decision-maker videos; groups and direct sends per `channels.md` |
| Captions | Burned in, always. Most shorts are watched on mute |
| Structure | Hook (0–3) → Signature (3–6) → Bridge (6–12) → one idea, shown (12–40) → CTA (last 5) |
| Idea count | Exactly one |

Plan long form so that shorts fall out of it; never the other way around.

### Short-form body patterns

- **The room.** Most people do X → what it costs (a number) → the turn → what we use, shown. (`02-hooks.md`)
- **Before / after.** The old way on screen, then the new way on screen. Both real.
- **Two screens.** The same thing appearing in two places (my screen, her phone) in the same second. Split screen or quick cut.
- **Walk the number.** Show the inputs, state the rule, show the result.
- **One job.** Trigger → someone acts → the thing happens → result → one human line about why it mattered.
- **The gap.** Someone (or a character) says the naive thing; the caption says the serious thing; the viewer holds both.
- **Text-led.** When the material is copy, not footage: designed text scenes carry the idea; one real visual anchors it (`11-text-scenes.md`). The default only for the launch type.

### Short-form CTA

One line, spoken and on screen. Never two asks. Pick from `channels.md` ("CTA per channel"); the CTA must match the primary channel of the video.

## Long form

| | |
|---|---|
| Length | 5–12 min; a full walkthrough may run to 15 |
| Aspect | 16:9 |
| Platforms | YouTube; a cut under 3 min for LinkedIn or direct sends |
| Structure | Cold open (0–10 s) → Signature → What you get → Body in chapters → CTA → end card to the next video |
| Chapters | Named in the description. Let viewers skip to what they need |

### Long-form body patterns

- **One job, start to finish.** Real time compressed, real numbers, the mess included. The flagship format for any business with a job to show.
- **One guide, properly.** Evergreen, search-friendly; the thing on screen plus a short explainer segment.
- **Why I built it.** Origin, honest about limits, with footage or stills of the problem.
- **A day with [person or character].** POV with cuts to the people around them.

## The set from one source

A shoot day, a long recording, a launch, or a batch of screen recordings is the scarce thing. Plan the whole set before building so nothing needs a reshoot, and brief only the cuts the material supports.

| Output | What it is | Goes to |
|---|---|---|
| **Long form** (1) | The walkthrough or guide, 16:9, chapters | YouTube |
| **Shorts** (3–5) | One idea each, own hook and CTA, cut from the same material | Own channels; groups where the CTA fits |
| **Language cut** (1, where `channels.md` names one) | Subtitled version of the group-facing short | The groups and organisations in that language |
| **Decision-maker cut** (1, when relevant) | Under 3 min, a human sets the frame, "talk to [owner]" | LinkedIn; direct to organisations |
| **Stills** (3–5) | Poster frames, the result, the screen, the place | Captions, posts, the website, decks |
| **Direct post** (1, when `channels.md` has a direct channel) | One still or 15 s clip, around a moment the recipient cares about | Direct to the person and their network |

Log what the source actually gave in the video's folder, and brief only what it supports.

## Two layers (character and technical videos)

When the speaker is a character, a junior, or anyone who should not carry the technical weight, plan two columns in the brief:

| Layer | Carried by | Example |
|---|---|---|
| What is actually happening | lower-third captions in a plain technical style, screen recordings, a human's line | `Phone GPS 3 m off. RTK ±2 cm.` |
| What the speaker thinks is happening | the speaker's voice; their captions in their colour | "Everyone can see me again. Good." |

Technical captions use a fixed quiet style (small, bottom third, no animation) so they read as fact. The other layer uses the brand or character colour. Plan one moment per video where the layers touch.

## Production notes

- Shoot the signature line clean, in several places, once. Build a library; do not reshoot it every video.
- Shoot hooks on the day, several options, same framing.
- Record screens natively (phone and desktop screen recording), and sync by a visible event.
- Name raw footage by date and backlog ID; log it in the video's folder and in `assets/ASSETS.md`.
- Light and season are a constraint and a gift; plan b-roll days apart from job days. On a job day the job comes first.
- Nothing in frame that cannot be published: faces without consent, customer data, licence plates, prices that will change. The gap protocol has a decision for each.
