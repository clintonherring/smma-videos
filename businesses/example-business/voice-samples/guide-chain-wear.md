# Chain wear (from northsidecycles.example/guides/chain-wear, written by Jonas)

Your chain stretches. Not the metal, the pins and the rollers wear and the links sit further apart. Once it is past a certain point it starts reshaping the teeth on the cassette, and then a new chain skips on the old cassette. That is the expensive one.

The number I use is 0.5 % on an 11-speed or 12-speed, 0.75 % on anything with fewer gears. I measure it with a gauge. Takes about ten seconds. You can buy one for about twelve euros and do it yourself on a Sunday.

Replace the chain on time and the cassette lasts about three chains. Leave it and you buy both. Chain from €25, fitted. Cassette from €45, plus the chain, because by then the chain is done too.

Nobody tells you this when you buy the bike. Now you know. If you are not sure, bring it in, I'll put the gauge on it while you wait and show you the part.
