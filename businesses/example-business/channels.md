## CTA per channel

One ask per video, matched to the channel. A knowledge video posted into a group gets a soft ask; the same cut on own channels gets the booking ask.

| Channel | Ask | Where it points |
|---|---|---|
| Own channels | "Book it on the site" / "The full check is on the site" | `northsidecycles.example/book?from=V##` or the guide page |
| Commuter group | "Ask me anything below" | the comments; the guide page only if asked |
| Club group | "The guide's on the site if you want the numbers" | guide page |
| Direct sends | none spoken; the message carries the link | the guide page |
| YouTube | "Prices and booking in the description" | prices page, booking page |
