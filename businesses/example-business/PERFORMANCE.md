# Performance log

One row per published video, per channel. Fill it a week after publishing and again after a month; the second look is the one that matters for evergreen videos. Numbers are what the platform shows, not estimates. The one-line learning is the only column that changes what we make next.

Phase exit criteria and pillar metrics live in `calendar.md` and `pillars.md`; this log is where the evidence accumulates.

## Log

| Date | ID | Pillar | Channel | Views (1 wk / 1 mo) | Completion % | Saves / shares | Comments (and the one that mattered) | Result attributable (the one number from profile.md) | Learning (one line) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| | | | | | | | | | |

Rules:

- "Attributable" means the person said so, or the link was the one in that video's caption. Do not guess.
- The same cut in a group and on an own channel is two rows. Reach is borrowed in phase 1 and we need to know which borrowing worked.
- A video with no row a month after publishing has not been published; it has been uploaded.

## If a video is not working

Check in this order. Each symptom points at one part of the structure; fix that part, not the whole video. One fix per re-cut; log the second version as its own row.

| What the numbers say | What is probably wrong | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Nobody finishes: completion drops in the first 3–6 s | The hook is about the product, not the viewer's moment, or the first frame is not the hook footage | Re-cut with an alternate hook from the brief (they were kept for this) |
| Finishes, but no shares or saves | The CTA, or more than one ask | One ask, spoken and on screen in the last 5 s; match it to the channel |
| Watches to the end but does not act | The product shows up too late, or a knowledge video is wearing a product CTA | Move the product shot earlier, or soften the CTA and let the action come from the next video |
| Group members react badly | The peer line: wrong viewer, jargon, a hint of selling | Use the right signature variant; remove the pitch for group posts |
| Views stop within days, no second life | Not evergreen or not findable | Title with the question a viewer would type; publish evergreen rows in the slot before the season |
| Text-scene video gets views, no reaction | Text scenes carried the whole video; nothing real was shown | Provide the shot the gap report asked for; rebuild with it |
| Good numbers everywhere, nothing attributable | Measurement, not the video | Video-specific link in the caption; ask the next enquiry where they saw it |

## Review once a period

Answers to the questions in `pillars.md` and `calendar.md`, dated, newest first.

### <period>

1. Which pillar got more or less than its share, and was that a choice?
2. Which "working when" column moved?
3. Which series got a "when is the next one"?
4. What did the period give us that is not in the backlog?
5. Did any video move a phase exit number?
6. Which gap or take in `seeds.md` is still unused?
