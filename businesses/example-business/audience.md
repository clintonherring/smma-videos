# Audience

Filled by `define-audience` from `profile.md` and the shop's own posts. Feeds channels, ideas, pillars and every hook: a pillar is defined by its viewer, and an idea is a moment that viewer has lived.

## The one viewer

Mara, 34, rides 6 km each way to work on a seven-year-old hybrid she bought second hand. She has never measured a chain and does not know what a cassette is until the bill names one. She watches on her phone on the train home, sound off. She is not interested in bikes; she is interested in the bike working on Monday and not being talked down to when it does not.

## Moments they have lived

- The chain skips under load pulling away from the lights, once, then again next week.
- A grinding noise that went away when it rained and came back when it dried.
- The quote that said "chain and cassette" and cost almost twice what she expected.
- Looking at a chain-wear gauge in a shop and not knowing which side goes in.
- Pumping tyres at the station pump and wondering if that number is right.
- Brakes that squeal in the wet and a friend saying "they all do that".
- The bike that stood in the hallway all winter and would not shift in March.
- A mechanic explaining something fast while she nodded and understood none of it.
- Leaving a bike for "a service" and not knowing what was actually done.
- Being handed a worn part and finally seeing what the noise was.

## What they already believe

Bikes are supposed to just work; parts wear out all at once and expensively; mechanics upsell; anything with a number is for enthusiasts. Across a table the objection is: "I don't want to become a bike person, I just want it fixed, and I want to know it needed fixing."

## The feeling at the end

- How-to and explainer videos: **capable** ("I can check this myself on Sunday in thirty seconds").
- Story and reveal videos: **recognised** ("that is exactly my bike").
- Invitation and offer videos: **welcome** ("I can turn up with my hallway bike and not be judged").

## What they would never forgive

Jargon without the thing in frame (no "drivetrain" unless the chain and the cogs are on screen); being treated as careless for not knowing; a video that is really an ad for a service she did not ask about; an invented number ("saves you hundreds").

## A second viewer?
