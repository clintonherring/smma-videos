# Profile: Northside Cycle Repair

A fictional one-person bike workshop used as the worked example for this repo. Every fact, number and URL in this folder is invented for the example; the `.example` domain does not resolve. The files are filled the way the guides would fill them, so a new business can see what "ready" looks like.

## What we do

Northside Cycle Repair fixes bikes for people who ride them every day: commuters, parents with cargo bikes, a few weekend road riders. One mechanic (Jonas), one bench, a small shop on the north side of a mid-sized Dutch city. Repairs, services and the parts that go with them; no bike sales.

## Who it is for

- The daily commuter whose bike is transport, not a hobby: it has to work on Monday.
- Parents running a cargo bike or child seat, who notice noises and ignore them until something gives.
- Weekend road riders who do some of their own wrenching and bring in the jobs they do not trust themselves with.

The *one viewer* is settled in `audience.md`: the commuter.

## The offer

| | |
|---|---|
| Products / services | Repairs by the job; a basic service and a full service; chain, cassette, brake pad and tyre replacements with parts; a free chain check on Sundays with any service (from October 2026) |
| Price or pricing shape | Fixed prices per job, posted in the shop and on the site (exact figures in `facts.md`) |
| What makes it different | The customer is shown the worn part and the number that says it is worn before anything is replaced |
| Where we operate | One shop, north side of the city; drop-in Tuesday to Saturday, Sunday mornings for checks |

## What a video should cause

Primary: a booking through the site (service or repair). Secondary: the viewer checks their own chain and comes in before the cassette is gone; a share into the local commuter group.

## The one number

Bookings made through the link in a video's caption (the booking page has a `?from=video` variant per video). Measured on the booking page's own counter, read once a week.
