# Hook bank

Hooks for Northside Cycle Repair. Rules in `strategy/02-hooks.md`. Each line is about Mara's moment, is specific, and could be said to one person across the bench. Tagged with the backlog ID when used; alternates kept for reposts.

## By type

### Pain you recognise

- "That skip when you pull away from the lights? That's your chain." (V01)
- "Your brakes squeal in the wet and someone told you they all do that." (V05)

### Contrarian

- "Your chain is a wear item. Nobody told you, so here's the thirty-second check." (V03, chosen)
- "A chain doesn't break. It stretches, quietly, until the cassette pays for it." (V04)

### The default (most people do X; it costs them Y; we don't)

- "Most people replace a cassette because nobody told them to replace the chain." (V03, alternate)

### Specific result

- "Thirty seconds on a Sunday saves you about forty-five euros." (V03, alternate)
- "Three chains per cassette, if you change them on time. One, if you don't." (V04)

### Did you know

- "The bike that stood in your hallway all winter wore out standing still." (V08)

### Unfinished action

- "This is the part. Hold on." (V02, before the worn pad comes into frame)

### Question you want answered

- "What do you actually get for thirty-nine euros?" (V06)
- "What happens if I just turn up on a Sunday?" (V07, alternate)

### Launch (V07)

- "Sunday chain checks. Free with any service." (V07, chosen; "free" alone would misstate facts #8)
