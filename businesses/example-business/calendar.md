# Calendar

Filled by `define-calendar` from `pillars.md` (shares), `channels.md` (one short a week) and `BACKLOG.md` (which ideas need a season). Feeds `SCHEDULE.md`.

A bike shop has a season: autumn wet, winter standstill, spring restart, summer distance. Slots follow those where an idea needs them; otherwise a plain four-week cadence. Shoot in season, publish before it.

## Slots

| Slot | When | What is happening for the viewer | What to make | What to publish |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cadence wk 1–4 | every four weeks, rolling | nothing seasonal; the bike is in daily use | evergreen Workshop knowledge and Show the part shorts (V01, V03, V04, V02) | one short a week, Thursday evening |
| October launch | first two weeks of October 2026 | the Sunday checks start | V07 launch | the Sunday before the first check, then once more |
| Autumn wet | October–November | brakes squeal; first dark commutes | V05 comparison; story 3 footage | early October, before the rain |
| Winter | December–February | bikes stand still; fewer walk-ins | V06 walkthrough (long), shot on quiet days | February, before the restart |
| Spring restart | March | the hallway bike comes out and will not shift | V08 story (shoot in March, publish within the month) | late March |

## Phases

Nobody is subscribed yet; reach has to be borrowed before it is owned. Exit criteria are plain numbers Jonas can see.

| Phase | What we make | Where it goes | Exit when |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Borrowed reach | Evergreen how-to, explainer, reveal and problem-and-fix shorts; the V07 launch | own channels, the commuter group (soft ask), direct sends after jobs | 6 shorts published; each in at least 2 places; first booking with `?from=V##` |
| 2. Own channels | "This is the part" and "Sunday check" episodes; the pairs | own channels carry weight; still shared into groups | 6 episodes; one viewer asking for the next one |
| 3. Long form and the people who decide | V06 walkthrough; an offer explainer on prices | YouTube; linked from the site; sent to the club | one long form watched past the "what it does not include" chapter; one booking that mentions it |

## Rules

- Any idea that needs footage from a season sits in that slot. Do not brief it out of season unless the assets already exist.
- Phase gates which backlog rows are *eligible*; pillar share and slot pick among them.
- Phase 3 videos may be shot early (quiet winter days) but are built and published when their phase arrives.
