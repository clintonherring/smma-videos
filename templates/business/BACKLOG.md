# Backlog

Ideas only, one line each. Nothing here is a brief; a brief is written in `make-video` when an idea is picked. First fill by `seed-ideas` (proposed from profile, audience, facts, channels and the site; the owner strikes and adds). After that, `video-schedule` adds a row whenever the owner has an idea.

Status: `idea → briefed → gaps-resolved → built → published` (plus `on hold` for roadmap-gated ideas). IDs: `V##` by default; a second track gets its own letter (set in `audience.md`).

Pillar, Slot and Phase columns are filled by `define-pillars` and `define-calendar` (first sort). Assets: `have` / `partial` / `none` from `assets/ASSETS.md`.
