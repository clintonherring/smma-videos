# Pillars

Filled by `define-pillars`. Needs `BACKLOG.md` (ideas to sort) and `audience.md` (a pillar is defined by its viewer). Feeds `calendar.md` (shares per slot) and the picking rule in `video-schedule`.

A pillar has one viewer, one job, a share of the mix and a way to tell it is working. Three to five. The backlog IDs under each are the first sort; a pillar with nothing under it is a sign the pillar or the ideas are not clear yet.

## Rules

- One pillar per video. If a video serves two, pick the one whose viewer it is written for; the other is the cross-over.
- Shares are a period's mix, not a weekly quota. Check them once a period (see `PERFORMANCE.md`), not per video.
- When a pillar is under its share and has assets available, it gets the next slot.
- A new idea with no pillar is a vetting question, not a blocker.

## Review questions (once a period)

1. Which pillar got more than its share? Less? Choice or accident of assets?
2. Which pillar's "working when" column actually moved?
3. What did the period give us that is not in the backlog?
