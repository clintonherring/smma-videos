# Audience

Filled by `define-audience`. Needs `profile.md`. Feeds channels, ideas, pillars and every hook: a pillar is defined by its viewer, and an idea is a moment that viewer has lived.

## The one viewer

[Owner] One person, watching alone on a phone. Who they are, what they do, what they are in the middle of when the video reaches them. Not a segment, not "small business owners".

## Moments they have lived

[Owner] Five to ten moments this person recognises in one line each. These become hooks.

## What they already believe

[Owner] What they think about the problem and about offers like this one. Includes the objection they would raise across a table.

## The feeling at the end

[Owner] The one feeling a video should leave them with, per kind of video if it differs (capable / recognised / relieved / curious / invited).

## What they would never forgive

[Owner] Jargon level (what they know and what would insult them), tone they would switch off, claims they would not believe.

## A second viewer?

[Owner] Is there a second person worth a second track (the buyer vs the user; the tenant vs the landlord)? If yes, repeat the sections above for them in one paragraph each. If no, say so.
