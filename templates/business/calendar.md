# Calendar

Filled by `define-calendar`. Needs `pillars.md` (shares), `channels.md` (cadence) and `BACKLOG.md` (which ideas need footage or a season). Feeds `SCHEDULE.md`.

The calendar decides what to make *when* better than a posting rhythm does. If the business has a season, key dates or launches, the slots follow those; if not, slots are a plain cadence (weeks or months). Shoot in season, publish before it, where that applies.

## Phases

Nobody is subscribed yet; reach has to be borrowed before it is owned. Exit criteria are plain numbers the owner can see.

| Phase | What we make | Where it goes | Exit when [Owner] |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Borrowed reach | Evergreen, safe-to-share videos | Posted into existing communities and sent directly, not just own channels | n videos published; each in at least 2 places; first attributable result |
| 2. Own channels | Series episodes; the pairs | Own channels carry some weight; still shared into groups | n episodes; a viewer asking for the next one |
| 3. Long form and the people who decide | Walkthrough, origin, offer explainer | Long-form channel; professional network; direct sends | one long form watched past its limits chapter; one conversation referencing a video |

## Rules

- Any idea that needs footage from a season or an event sits in that slot. Do not brief it out of season unless the assets already exist.
- Phase gates which backlog rows are *eligible*; pillar share and slot pick among them.
- Phase 3 videos may be shot early but are built and published when their phase arrives.
