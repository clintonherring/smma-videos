# Voice samples

Unedited writing by the person who speaks for the business, one file per sample, saved here when the owner agrees (`business-voice` mode 1). Pages they wrote themselves, emails to customers, quotes, captions from their own channel, group posts. Nothing a copywriter or an agency wrote for them.

`npx smma-videos extract-text <file-or-url>` turns a `.docx`, `.pdf`, `.html` or URL into plain text; `npx smma-videos voice-profile voice-samples/*.md` profiles all of them at once. The profile lives in `../voice.md`.
