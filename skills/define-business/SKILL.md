# Define the business

Upstream: nothing but what the person gives you.
Downstream: audience, facts, voice, channels, brand.
Why now: the one viewer, the facts worth collecting, the channels that matter and the CTA all follow from what is offered and to whom.

Shared shape and rules: `strategy/12-guides.md` (evidence before questions, `[Owner]` proposals, refine by correction, finish with readiness).

## Before asking anything

1. `npx smma-videos readiness <slug>` (run `init` first if the folder is missing). This guide has no upstream, so go straight on.
2. Read what exists: `profile.md` as it is; any URL (home page, about, pricing, one product or service page), text, document or post the person gave; the name of the business if that is all there is.
3. Infer and state, in five lines, what you can already fill: what they do, for whom, the offer, where, what a video would probably be for. Say "I read X and Y; here is what I think the business is. Correct me in a word."

If there is nothing to read, ask for the one thing that gives the most: a URL, or "describe it to me the way you would to someone at a party".

## Questions (two or three a turn, with options)

Ask only what reading did not answer:

- **What should a video cause?** install / book / call / enquire / visit / sign up / share. "If one video worked perfectly, what would someone do five minutes later?"
- **The one number.** "What would you look at to know it worked: bookings through the link, installs that week, enquiries that mention it, group members?" Where is that number visible?
- **What makes it different**, in the customer's words, not a claim. "What do customers say when they recommend you?"
- **Where you operate**, if not obvious: a place, a region, online, a language.
- **Price shape** only as a shape (subscription, per job, tiers, free for some). Exact figures go to `facts.md`.

## Write the file

`profile.md` with the sections the manifest requires: `## What we do`, `## Who it is for`, `## The offer`, `## What a video should cause`, `## The one number`, plus `## Sources` and `## Needs the owner`. Remove the template marker line. Plain sentences, no slogan. Anything inferred but unconfirmed stays marked `[Owner]`; anything only the owner can know (the number, the outcome) is `[Owner]` until they say it.

## Refine by correction

When the person corrects a line, rewrite that section and say what moved ("changed the outcome from installs to bookings; the one number is now bookings through the caption link; that changes the CTA options in channels later").

## Finish

- `npx smma-videos readiness <slug>`; show it.
- Say the next one and why: "Next: audience, because a pillar is defined by its viewer and an idea is a moment that viewer has lived. Shall we?"
- Offer the one-pass option if a site exists: "While I'm reading your site for the audience, I can pull the facts and how you write at the same time (collect-facts and business-voice)."

## Do not

- Ask for a mission statement, values, or brand personality. Not used.
- Write marketing copy into the profile. It is a description for the agent, in the owner's words.
- Leave the outcome and the one number blank without `[Owner]`; they drive the CTA and the performance log.
