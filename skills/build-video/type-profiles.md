# Build profiles per video type

The type (settled in the brief, `strategy/08-video-types.md`) fixes how the composition is paced and dressed. There are no tone presets: the business's `voice.md` sets the register, the type sets the rhythm. Durations are for a short unless stated. Holds are *settled* holds (`strategy/07-edit-fundamentals.md` reading floor): labels ~0.8 s, sentences ~0.3 s/word (min 1.2 s), text scenes +0.5 s.

| Type | Beats | Holds | Transitions | Captions | Text scenes: role and entrance | Narration default | Music |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **How-to** | 3–5 | comfortable; the step on screen holds until the viewer could do it | soft cut; match cut into the screen or the hands | every spoken line; the rule once as a caption too | frame around the step: a step card (number small, rule one line) that slides in from the step's side and out again; centrepiece only when degraded | on-camera voice; narration over a screen recording when nobody is in frame | none |
| **Comparison** | 4–6 | punchy; one claim per beat; each side held long enough to read its ceiling | hard cut between sides; split or wipe at the turn | the ceiling of each side as a caption on its footage | mirrored side cards (name, ceiling); the verdict line as a slam, then hold | on-camera voice | none |
| **Problem and fix** | 4–6 | the cost slams and holds; the fix flows (entry → key action → result) without text over the key action | hard cut from the moment to the cost; cut on action through the fix | spoken lines; the limit as a small caption near the end | the cost as a number card (counter or slam, unit small); the limit as a quiet fade card; never over the key action | on-camera voice | none |
| **Reveal** | 4–5 | the thing holds; text does not; nothing over the key action | cut on the action; the result settles before any card | spoken lines only during entry and result | one line on what it means, fade, after the result | on-camera or narrated; captions only is acceptable | none |
| **Launch** | 4–6 | text-forward: hook card holds longest; highlight cards each at the floor, then the full set | clean slides and wipes; the one real visual gets a hard reveal | few; the cards carry the words | the centrepiece: hook (type-on or slam), name + the real visual, 2–3 highlight cards sequential, punchline; signature optional and short | captions only, or a short narrated line | owner-supplied licensed bed allowed, low, fades under the punchline |
| **Walkthrough (long)** | chapters | breathing room; the cold open holds; numbers as lower thirds held to the floor | cut on action between viewpoints; chapter title as a clean wipe | spoken lines throughout; the day's numbers as lower thirds | chapter titles; the day's numbers; nothing else | on-camera voice; narration to bridge gaps | none, or an owner-supplied bed under the cold open only |
| **Origin / why (long)** | the five moves, then chapters | restrained; the abstract card holds long; the person's face holds | slow fades; clean cuts within chapters | spoken lines | the abstract once (fade, long hold); chapter titles; nothing during the person's telling | the person's own voice, on camera or narrated over stills | none |
| **Story** | 3–5 | warm; the human line holds past the floor; the moment plays without interruption | soft cuts; time order; no wipes | spoken lines; the human line as a caption and as a pull-quote card if the footage lacks it | the human line as a pull-quote over a still, fade in, held; nothing else | the person telling it, or narrated; never a generated voice imitating a real person | none |
| **Explainer** | 3–5 | restrained; technical captions held to the floor; the mechanism card held | clean cuts; no chaos | technical captions in the quiet style (small, bottom third, no animation) carry the real names and numbers | at most one mechanism card (diagram or list, revealed one by one, then held) | narrated, or a human sets the frame on camera then narration | none |
| **Invitation** | 3–5 | warm; the place and the people hold; the list revealed one by one | soft slides | spoken lines | "what you'd do" as a 3-item list, soft slide, revealed sequentially and held | on-camera voice from the person who will be there | none |
| **Offer (long)** | sections | each tier card held; "does not include" lines held | slides; hold the full set of tiers | spoken lines | tier cards, sequential, each held; "does not include" lines as quiet cards | the owner's voice | none |

## Two layers (character or junior speaker)

When `voice.md` "Who speaks" names a character: the character's captions use the brand or character colour and its register; technical captions use the quiet style and the business's plain register; plan one moment where the two touch (the serious human line, the character's miss), and give that moment the longest hold in the body.

## Format defaults

| | 9:16 short | 16:9 long | 1:1 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Canvas | 1080×1920 | 1920×1080 | 1080×1080 |
| Caption position | lower third, above the bottom 15 % | lower third | lower quarter |
| Text-scene type scale | display 88–104 px, support 40–48 px | display 96–120 px, support 44–52 px | display 80–96 px |
| Safe zones | centre 80 % horizontally; avoid top 10 % and bottom 15 % | 5 % margins | 8 % margins |

`brand.md` overrides any of these.
