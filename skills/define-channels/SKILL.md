# Define the channels

Upstream: business (`profile.md`), audience (`audience.md`).
Downstream: ideas, pillars, calendar, every CTA.
Why now: an idea for a community group and an idea for LinkedIn are different ideas, and the calendar cannot set a cadence without knowing how many channels and how much time there is.

Shared shape: `strategy/12-guides.md`. Method: `strategy/05-ctas-and-publishing.md`, `strategy/09-which-videos.md` §3 and §6.

## Before asking anything

1. `npx smma-videos readiness <slug>`. If `profile.md` or `audience.md` is `missing`: "I need the audience first, because channels are where that viewer already is; shall we do audience now, or go on with the channels marked `[Owner]`?" If `draft`, go on and name the open items.
2. Read `profile.md` (what a video should cause → the asks), `audience.md` (the viewer → where they already are), the site's footer and social links, any groups or communities the person mentioned.
3. Propose: "Your viewer is X; people like that are in A and B. Your outcome is bookings, so the ask on own channels is 'book', and in a group it has to be 'read the guide' or the post gets removed. Here is a first channel table." Mark the groups `[Owner: which]`.

## Questions (two or three a turn)

- **Where is the viewer already?** "Which groups, forums, newsletters, local pages, trade communities does that person actually read? Which ones are you already in?" Reach is borrowed before it is owned (`09-which-videos.md` §3); own channels are the archive at first.
- **Who carries it in?** "For each group or organisation: who would share a video of yours there? You, a member, a customer, a partner?" A channel with nobody to carry it is a repost, not a channel.
- **The ask per channel.** Confirm the table: product ask on own channels, soft ask in groups, "talk to [owner]" on LinkedIn, share ask for the public. Never price as an ask.
- **Language.** "Any channel where the viewer reads a different language? Subtitles at minimum there."
- **Cadence, honestly.** "How many videos a month can you actually ship, including collecting the clips? How much time for posting and replying?" Write what they say, not what a platform recommends. Pick the **primary channel** the schedule plans for.

## Write the file

`channels.md` with `## Channels` (table: channel, who is there, what goes there, how it gets there / shared by, language), `## CTA per channel` (ask, where it points), `## Cadence` (videos per period, time for shooting, time for posting, primary channel), `## Distribution line` (the pattern), `## Needs the owner`. Remove the template marker. Specific groups and people stay `[Owner]` until named.

## Refine by correction

When a group turns out to forbid product posts, change its ask to the soft one and say which backlog ideas that affects. When cadence drops, say what the calendar will do about it (fewer slots, not thinner videos).

## Finish

- `npx smma-videos readiness <slug>`; show it.
- "Next: ideas, because pillars are a sort of real ideas, and now that I know the viewer, the facts and the channels I can propose ten to twenty one-liners for you to strike and add to."

## Do not

- Recommend a posting frequency. Ask what is sustainable and plan for that.
- List a platform because it exists. A channel needs the viewer in it and someone to carry the video there.
- Put a product ask on a group video.
